# Commuter: Abu Dhabi concept demonstration

## Purpose

Introduce Commuter to the Abu Dhabi Department of Transport as a ride-booking
experience for discussion. This is a product demonstration, not an official
Department of Transport service, partnership, or endorsement.

## What Commuter does

Commuter helps a traveler plan a trip by choosing a pickup point, destination,
arrival time, and private or shared ride option. Before confirming, the traveler
can review the trip details and fare. The app then keeps trip status and
driver-passenger updates together through the journey.

Ride and vehicle choices can vary by location and operating arrangements. The
Abu Dhabi route shown in the demo is illustrative; it does not promise service
coverage, availability, journey time, or a particular fare.

## A simple story to present

1. A traveler plans a journey across Abu Dhabi.
2. They enter where they are going and when they need to arrive.
3. They compare the private and shared ride choices shown for that trip.
4. They review the trip details, confirm, and follow trip updates.

## Points to explore with the Department

- Where a ride-booking experience could complement existing mobility options.
- Which types of journeys and traveler groups would be most useful to support.
- What operating, accessibility, service-area, and coordination requirements
  would need to be agreed before any deployment.

## Presentation guardrails

- Describe the Abu Dhabi screen as a concept demonstration.
- Do not imply Department approval, partnership, or integration.
- Do not promise citywide coverage, guaranteed pickup times, savings, safety
  outcomes, or fares without independently verified evidence.
- Treat routes, vehicle availability, and prices in sample screens as examples,
  not live service information.
